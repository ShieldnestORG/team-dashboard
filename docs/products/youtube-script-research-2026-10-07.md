## Six findings
1. **V** Every title, hook and "credibility" line comes from two small prompts with no facts and no validator. The one-line angle prompt writes the title (`content-strategy.ts:213`; published title = stored angle, case aside, in 3 of 3 samples). The script prompt forces `credibility`, "Always use 2026" and "3-4 sections" (`script-writer.ts:189,204,216`), so the model invents credentials ("our research", "a model that actually works"), experiences ("I Tested 5... Paid My Rent") and promises. Nothing checks output, and a filler template can ship (`:171-176,246-326`).
2. **V** Facts are the crux, and the same failure is already measured next door: the TX comparison cron fed Cosmos Hub numbers to the model labelled TX (180 validators vs 55 bonded; `Tx Eco System/OWNER-DECISIONS-2026-10-05.md` U1; code `content-crons.ts:396-410`). Fix: code builds a FACTS packet and inserts every number as a placeholder; the model never types a digit.
3. **V** The first 30 s is filler: 88 narrated words (about 33 s, matching the measured first section at 0:33) before any content, opening "Welcome back to Tokns.fi" on a 21-subscriber channel. YouTube's Intro metric is retention at 30 s.
4. **V** The topic engine is a closed 30-topic pool (unchanged since 2026-04-16) with a 20-row memory. In the newest 60 videos 28 of 30 topics ran, 25 of them 2+ times, 7 of them 3 times, plus one exact duplicate title. Weights are random because `yt_keyword_performance` and `yt_analytics` are empty. At 3-5 videos a day the memory is 4-7 days and the pool cycles every 6-10 days, the "mass-produced, repetitive" pattern YouTube's policy names.
5. **V** Lines are written as paragraphs but shown as bullets: 6 of 17 fixture lines exceed 120 chars (cut with "..." on the slide), 7 have 2-3 sentences. Add a parallel `onScreen[]` (max 7 words) plus the spoken-line rules in sections 6 and 7.
6. **V** Money-adjacent promises ("Ready to Explode", "Which One Actually Makes You Rich?", "Next Major Wealth Cycle") sit close to what YouTube's Spam policy prohibits (titles that do not deliver, get-rich-quick promotion; a risk, not a ruling) and are in tension with the owner's own TX facts (about $15M cap, accounts growing linearly, `KNOWLEDGE.md` §7, 2026-10-02/03). The operator runs a TX validator, so that connection should be spoken in the video (FTC influencer guidance; not legal advice).

## 6. Proposed script format
Principles: one spoken string = one beat that must stand alone; numbers come from code; screen text is not the transcript. Mapping (beats as in A@8ca93c54):
| Field | Beat | Words | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hook.text` | title card | 12-25 | concrete claim, question or scene from FACTS; restates the title promise; no greeting. `hook.type` is code-picked and rotates: question, number, myth, scenario, change |
| `introduction.greeting` | merged into first intro beat | 0-6 | "This is Coherence Daddy." or "". Drop "welcome back"/"what's up" (changes A's `DEFAULT_GREETING` and its test) |
| `topicIntro` | intro 1 | 15-25 | the question + the N parts in order |
| `valueProposition` | intro 2 | "" or 20 | only if it names the specific payoff |
| `credibility` | intro 3 | "" or 20 | repurposed as provenance: "Numbers are from the chain, checked {{asof}}." Never credentials |
| `section.title` | section beat | 2-6 | speakable, no colon or symbols; `type` in fact, step, myth, example, compare, risk, takeaway |
| `content[i]` | content beat | 8-22 | one idea, 1-2 sentences, no digits outside placeholders |
| `onScreen[i]` (new) | bullet text | max 7 | label + placeholder number, or a noun phrase; same length as `content` |
| `recap` | one beat | 2-3 items of max 10 | only if the video is 4 min or longer; new wording |
| `finalThought` | quote beat | max 22 | calls back to the hook; no "journey, not a destination" |
| `callToAction` | one beat | max 18 each | `subscribe` = verb + object + reason; `like` = ""; `comment` = topic question, never about holdings; `nextVideo` = "" unless a real video is supplied; optional `midCta` after section 2 |
| code-inserted | own beats | fixed | CONNECTION within 30 s on TX/tokns videos; NOT_ADVICE before the CTA |

## 7. DRAFT prompts
**System** (static):
```
You write narration for the Coherence Daddy YouTube channel (@coherencedaddy). A cloned human voice reads every "content" line aloud, one line per slide. Return ONE JSON object, nothing else.
WHO WE ARE: Coherence Daddy builds private self-help tools and teaches real skills. We also build tokns.fi and run a validator on the TX chain, so we are not neutral about TX and never pretend to be. Describe a product only with sentences from BRAND_FACTS; if it is not there, you do not know it.
VOICE: confident, direct, plain, willing to be uncomfortable. A smart friend explaining one thing, not a guru, not hype. Contractions are fine. "We" = the organisation, "you" = the viewer, "I" only for a stated opinion, never for something that happened to you.
RULES (breaking one gets the script thrown away):
1. NUMBERS COME FROM CODE. Never type a digit. Every number, date, price, percentage or ranking is a placeholder like {{tx.bonded}} taken from FACTS. You may spell counts of your own parts ("three steps"); only the title may use a digit, for a counted promise. A hypothetical example uses a FACTS entry of kind "hypothetical" and its sentence starts with "Say" or "Imagine". No FACT, no number.
2. NO INVENTED LIFE. You did not test, buy, trade, earn, interview or research anything. Never write "I tested", "we found", "our research", "my portfolio", "in my experience", "studies show", "experts say". Name only sources listed in FACTS.
3. NO ADVICE, NO PREDICTIONS. Never tell the viewer to buy, sell, hold, stake or allocate money to a named asset. Never say what a price will do. Explain how it works, what the data shows, what can go wrong, what we do not know.
4. THE TITLE IS A CONTRACT. Write "promise": one sentence on what the viewer will know at the end, and deliver all of it. If the title counts items, deliver exactly that many, in order. Answer any title question inside the video.
5. START WITH THE POINT. No "welcome back", no "what's up", no "today we". hook.text is one concrete claim, question or scene, max 25 words. The only sign-on allowed is introduction.greeting: "This is Coherence Daddy." or empty. The first FACT is spoken before word 60.
6. ONE IDEA PER LINE. Each content line is 1-2 short spoken sentences, 8-22 words, commas and full stops only. No lists, brackets, parentheses, colons, URLs, emoji, stage directions or pronunciation notes. Write DeFi, TX, tokns.fi as normal text; code handles pronunciation.
7. THE SCREEN IS NOT A TRANSCRIPT. onScreen has one entry per content line: max 7 words, a label plus a placeholder number or a noun phrase, never the sentence.
8. SAY WHEN. A line with a time-sensitive fact says "as of {{asof}}" once per section. Never "this year", "recently" or "now" without a date.
9. Never mention evntrace (the system adds its line). Never call anything encrypted, anonymous, guaranteed or "the top". Never write the not-financial-advice or validator disclosure lines; code inserts them.
Obey FORMAT, HOOK_TYPE and LENGTH. Do not copy openings or title patterns from RECENT_TITLES. An empty string means "skip this beat".
```

## 8. Code gates (Rule 6: code decides, the model writes)
Order: LLM, parse, validate, substitute placeholders, A's `sanitizeScript`, `buildBeats`. Plumbing: `generateScript(strategy)` has no `db` handle (`script-writer.ts:167`, called at `production.ts:89`), so build FACTS in `production.ts` (or pass `db`) and carry them on new optional `ContentStrategy` fields (`content-strategy.ts:301-310`): `format`, `hookType`, `freshReason`, `facts`.
1. Parse and shape (`onScreen.length == content.length`, budgets). Failure: one repair call carrying the error, never the template.
2. Placeholders: any digit outside a placeholder is a violation (small whitelist: Web3, L1, L2); an unknown id is a violation; a `hypothetical` id outside a sentence starting "Say" or "Imagine" is a violation; code substitutes display values (pre-rounded, with "about"). Code cannot verify prose claims, hence the review card in item 8.
3. Regex bans: greeting patterns; `\bI (tested|tried|made|earned|lost|bought|sold)\b`; "our research|we found|we tested|my portfolio|studies show|experts say"; "pronounced"; the DESIGN.md §7 list; prediction words (will explode/hit/reach, guaranteed, 100x, wealth cycle, financial freedom); "mid-cap|blue chip" unless in FACTS; "the top"; "encrypted|anonymous" unless in BRAND_FACTS.
4. Title: max 65 chars; no year unless the format is dated; no power-word prefix or keyword suffix; counted promise equals delivered count; token-Jaccard against ALL past titles and angles below 0.5.
5. Budget by the formula, re-checked against measured TTS duration.
6. Up to 2 repairs sending only the violations; then fail the production (`error: script_validation`, alert). Deterministic fallback for the TX pillar: fixed sentences filled from FACTS, no LLM.
7. Persist `{script, facts, violations, scriptSource, model, promptVersion}` (`assets.scriptPath` exists) and `strategy.metadata` = {format, hookType, freshReason, factsAsOf}.
8. Review card for the owner on A's `review` status: title, promise, each line beside its fact id and source, duration, violations.

(true per brief 07 fact 4: TOKNS.FI validator, commission 0.12). NOT_ADVICE "This is education, not financial advice. Check the data yourself."
